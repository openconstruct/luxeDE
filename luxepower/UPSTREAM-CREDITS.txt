Upstream projects whose system interfaces LuxePower builds on. Both remain
separately packaged; LuxePower bundles no source from either project.

TLP
Source: https://linrunner.de/tlp and https://github.com/linrunner/TLP
Copyright: 2008-2024, Thomas Koch and TLP contributors
License: GPL-2+
TLP supplies the power-management command surface used by LuxePower
(tlp-stat, tlp start, /usr/share/tlp/defaults.conf, /etc/tlp.conf).
TLP 1.3 through 1.10 are supported.

TLPUI
Source: https://github.com/d4nj1/TLPUI
Copyright: 2018-2024, Daniel Pawlicki (d4nj1) and TLPUI contributors
License: GPL-2.0-or-later
TLPUI documents the tlp-stat command surface that guided LuxePower's TLP
interaction. TLPUI is not bundled and remains installed independently when
desired.
